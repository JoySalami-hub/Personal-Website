01. About me

A little about my journey

Hello, I’m Dami Salami, a Cloud Support Engineer working at Amazon. I’ve always enjoyed computers,and building things, largely because I love problem-solving and figuring out how things work. Recently, I’ve gotten more into tinkering and experimenting with technology outside of work. I’m currently in the process of building my own server, and eventually I’d love to create a mini cyberdeck. My interest in technology has taken me from studying Computer Science to working with the Longford County Council, then Unum, and eventually to my current role at Amazon.

Outside of technology, I’m a big fan of video games, art, and reading, with reading probably being my number one hobby. I love getting completely stuck into a good game, especially Zelda, which is my favourite game series. I also enjoy things like Sudoku and Murdoku, which probably says a lot about how much I enjoy puzzles and problem-solving. Overall, I’m someone who loves learning, experimenting, and getting stuck into new things, whether that’s building something with a computer, playing a game, creating art, or getting lost in a really good book.
